CropX combines in-ground soil sensors with satellite imagery and weather data to build a continuous picture of every block. Sensors are buried at depth and measure moisture, temperature, and salinity in real time, transmitting back to the platform via cellular or LoRa.

Yes. The CropX platform integrates with most major irrigation controllers, weather stations, and farm-management systems through native connectors and a public API. If you have a less common setup, our integrations team will scope a custom connection during onboarding.

Most growers see measurable changes in irrigation use within the first season — typically a 20–30% reduction once schedules are calibrated to the soil data. Yield improvements compound over time as the platform learns your fields and you fine-tune practices.

Every account includes onboarding sessions for your team, agronomist-led calibration during the first growing season, and ongoing access to our customer success team. Enterprise customers also get a named account manager and quarterly reviews.

You own your data — full stop. CropX stores it encrypted in transit and at rest, and we never share or sell it. Customers can export their full data history at any time, and our platform meets SOC 2 Type II and ISO 27001 standards.
