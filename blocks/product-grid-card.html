CropX Hardware

The sensors behind the data

Every CropX sensor is purpose-built for the field — rugged, wireless, and designed to deliver the data your agronomic decisions depend on.

CropX Evato 1 (ET1)

Actual evapotranspiration sensor. Measures real crop water use directly in the field for precise irrigation scheduling.
CropX Strato 1 (ST1)

All-in-one weather station. Captures temperature, humidity, wind, and solar radiation in a single compact unit.
CropX Vertex 4 (V4)

All-in-one smart soil sensor. Monitors moisture, temperature, and salinity at multiple depths simultaneously.
CropX Telemetry 2 (TD2)

Field data gateway. Aggregates sensor data from across the farm and transmits it reliably to the cloud.
CropX Rivo 1 (RG1)

Rain gauge. Logs precipitation events with high accuracy to inform irrigation decisions and water balance models.
CropX Apex 1

Precision soil sensor. Delivers high-resolution subsurface data to support optimal root-zone management decisions.
