Sensors & Devices

The sensors, stations, and gateways behind your farm data

Purpose-built for field conditions — every CropX device is designed to collect accurate data and keep collecting it season after season.

CropX Evato 1 (ET1)

Actual evapotranspiration sensor — measures real ET directly in the field for accurate irrigation scheduling.
CropX Strato 1 (ST1)

All-in-one weather station — air temperature, humidity, wind speed, and solar radiation in a single unit.
CropX Vertex 4 (V4)

All-in-one smart soil sensor — soil moisture, temperature, and EC at multiple depths.
CropX Gateway (GW3)

LoRaWAN gateway connecting field sensors back to the cloud — cellular, Wi-Fi, or satellite backhaul.
CropX Cable (CB1)

Multi-depth wired soil sensor array for permanent monitoring in high-value perennial crops.
CropX Repeater (RP1)

Range extender for large or complex fields — keeps sensors connected without additional gateways.
