Field-Ready Devices

CropX Hardware

Purpose-built sensors and gateways engineered for the harshest field conditions — and designed to work out of the box with the CropX platform.

Soil Probe  

Multi-depth capacitance probe measuring moisture, temperature, and EC at 15 cm intervals — installs in minutes, lasts years.
Weather Station  

Hyperlocal air temperature, humidity, wind, and rain gauge — paired with satellite forecasts for ET calculations down to the block level.
Field Gateway  

Solar-powered 4G/LTE gateway with a 10 km radio range — aggregates data from up to 50 probes and pushes to the cloud every 15 minutes.
Crop Scanner  

Handheld multispectral imager for canopy NDVI and chlorophyll index readings — syncs directly to the CropX app for instant field notes.
Irrigation Controller  

Smart valve controller that executes CropX irrigation schedules automatically — supports up to 24 zones with fail-safe manual override.
Flow Meter  

Ultrasonic in-line flow meter that logs water volumes per irrigation event — feeds directly into water-use reports and sustainability audits.
Leaf Wetness Sensor  

Detects surface moisture duration at canopy level to quantify disease-pressure hours — triggers fungicide timing alerts before infection windows open.
Dendrometer  

Measures micro-variations in trunk or stem diameter to detect plant water stress earlier than any soil sensor — ideal for tree crops and vines.
Solar Radiation Sensor  

Pyranometer-grade PAR and global radiation readings used to improve ET₀ calculations and optimise shading systems in protected cultivation.
EC / pH Probe  

In-line fertigation probe that measures electrical conductivity and pH in real time — alerts when nutrient solution drifts outside target range.
Field Camera  

Solar-powered 4G camera with time-lapse and motion capture — visual confirmation of crop development, pest pressure, and irrigation events from anywhere.
