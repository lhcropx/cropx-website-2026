Why Soil EC Matters More Than You Think in Drip Irrigation

Electrical conductivity isn't just a proxy for soil texture — it's the hidden variable that determines whether your drip tape is working with your soil or against it. Understanding EC variation within a field can mean the difference between an efficient irrigation event and a slow, costly leaching cycle that drains nutrients before roots can capture them.
